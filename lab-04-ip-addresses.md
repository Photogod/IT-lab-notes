# Lab 04: IP Addresses and Network Adapters

## What I found
- My laptop has more than one network connection.
- Wi-Fi: my real internet connection.
- Ethernet 2: a VirtualBox Host-Only adapter, a private lab network.
- Each connection has its own IP address.

## Key ideas
- An IP address belongs to a connection, not the whole computer.
- A subnet is a group of devices on the same local network.
- The subnet mask shows where the network part ends and the device part begins.
- The default gateway is the router, the exit to other networks.
